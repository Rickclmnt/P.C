ALIS Pilot Program

Run ALIS against a real matter before you commit to anything.

The pilot is a paid, time-boxed evaluation — three to four weeks, hosted on our infrastructure, against your firm's own real or sanitized matters. If you continue, the pilot fee is credited in full toward your first year, and ALIS moves on-premises onto infrastructure you control.

01 / HOW THE PILOT WORKS

Time-boxed. Provider-hosted. Fully credited if you stay.

Everything below reflects the actual pilot agreement, no separate marketing version of the terms.

Duration

3–4 weeks

Starts once ALIS is deployed and confirmed operational in your environment, ends on an agreed Pilot End Date.

Hosting

Provider-hosted during the pilot

ALIS runs on RICKCLMNT-controlled infrastructure for the pilot window. Choose sanitized or redacted matters accordingly.

Access

No feature gating

Full platform access within the agreed scope: practice area, attorney count, and active matters set at signing.

Fees

One-time, scaled to firm size

Invoiced at signing, sized to your applicable license tier and fully credited toward the first year if you continue.

Data

Confidential, then deleted

Matter data is never used beyond operating the pilot and is permanently deleted from our infrastructure after the pilot ends, or sooner on request.

Continuation

Opt out anytime before the deadline

Notify us before the cancellation deadline and the engagement simply ends — no further fees, no continued access.

ALIS / PILOT
If you continue, ALIS leaves our infrastructure — permanently.

Continuing past the pilot means redeployment on-premises, on infrastructure your firm controls, coordinated in advance so access isn't interrupted. From that point, matter data stays inside your environment at all times.
