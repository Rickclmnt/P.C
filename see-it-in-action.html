01 / MATTER INTELLIGENCE

From a pile of documents to a structured matter.

Ingestion, extracted facts, timeline, contradictions and deposition analysis on a real case file.

Ingest

Ingest Documents

The landing view for a matter — contracts, judgments, depositions, pleadings and correspondence all go through the same intake pipeline.

Case reconstruction

Extracted Facts

Every fact pulled from the record carries a source citation back to the document and page it came from.

Timeline

Timeline, with a flagged gap

Dated events are ordered into a chronology, with corroboration status and gaps surfaced rather than smoothed over.

Contradictions & gaps

A high-severity date conflict, caught

Conflicting accounts are quoted from both source documents side by side, with the reasoning trace showing how the conflict was found — and a guardrail flagging it for attorney review before proceeding.

Deposition intelligence

Transcripts

Structured facts, admissions and inconsistencies extracted from a deposition transcript.

02 / LEGAL INTELLIGENCE

Research and drafting, tied to sources.

Precedent research, citation verification and formal legal tests, each showing its reasoning trace.

Legal research

Legal Research

A question answered against the indexed case-law corpus, with cited precedents, a SAFE guardrail badge and the reasoning trace behind the answer.

Citation verification

Verify Citations

Citations checked against the underlying case-law corpus, flagging any authority where the case or proposition doesn't match.

Formal legal tests

Legal Tests

Each element of a defined legal test evaluated against the case facts separately, so every step is inspectable.

Drafting

Draft Documents

A first-pass draft assembled from the facts and templates on file — a starting point for the lawyer, not a final document.

03 / CONTRACT INTELLIGENCE

From clause to portfolio.

Risk scoring, redlines, deadline tracking and full-portfolio due diligence.

Clause risk analysis

Risk Report

Clauses extracted, classified and scored clause-by-clause against the firm's configured rules.

Redlines

Redlines, with reasoning per edit

Proposed replacement language drawn from approved clause templates, with the reasoning behind each suggested edit shown alongside it.

Portfolio intelligence

Portfolio view

Contract risk, obligations and renewal windows rolled up across the matter's full document set.

Deadlines

Deadlines, tracked automatically

Contractual deadlines pulled from the documents and tracked without manual calendaring.

M&A due diligence

Due Diligence

Diligence findings assembled across the full document set, with the rating scaling as portfolio size grows.

04 / STRATEGIC INTELLIGENCE

Outcome estimates you can take apart.

Weighted factor breakdowns and structured strategy — nothing asserted without a visible basis.

Outcome assessment

Outcome Prediction, factor by factor

A success likelihood built from explicit weighted factors — admission strength, corroboration rate, evidence gaps and more — each shown with its own weight and direction. When the margin is thin, ALIS says so instead of presenting false confidence.

Strategy builder

Legal Strategy

Key issues, arguments and counterarguments structured around the matter's actual facts and legal framework.

05 / FIRM INTELLIGENCE

Institutional knowledge, made searchable.

Firm knowledge base, client rollups, billing and configurable playbooks.

Institutional memory

Firm Knowledge Base

Search results across the firm's indexed memos, precedents and prior matters.

Client intelligence

Client Intelligence rollup

Multiple matters rolled into a single client-level view of risk and exposure.

Practice operations

Billing

Time entries and invoice generated from the matter activity ALIS already recorded.

Firm controls

Playbooks

Firm-specific standards configured once and applied consistently across every matter.

Firm controls

A contract, rescored against the playbook

The same contract re-evaluated once the firm's playbook is applied, showing the score move with the rule that moved it.
