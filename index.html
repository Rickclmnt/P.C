01 / WHAT SETS ALIS APART

Your record is the source of truth.

ALIS does not rely on a language model to generate a legal conclusion. It builds a structured representation of the matter, applies explicit rules and deterministic algorithms, and traces every conclusion back to the evidence that supports it.

ALIS / CORE
No language model generates the legal conclusion.

Drafts and redlines are assembled from approved templates and verified facts. Outcome assessments are calculated from explicit, weighted factors. Legal tests are broken down into their individual elements. Every result is designed to be inspectable, reproducible, and auditable.

02 / WHAT IT IS

A legal intelligence layer for your firm's record.

ALIS is designed around a simple constraint: every legal conclusion should be traceable back to the evidence and authorities from which it was derived.

01

Grounded

Facts and conclusions are tied to the documents and authorities available to the system, rather than invented from a blank context.

02

Auditable

Every material finding can point back to its source — down to the document and page where possible.

03

Private

ALIS is designed for self-hosted deployment, keeping sensitive matter data within the firm's own environment.

03 / WHAT IT DOES

From a pile of documents to a structured matter.

ALIS turns unstructured legal material into facts, timelines, authorities, conflicts and working drafts that lawyers can inspect.

01 / MATTER INTELLIGENCE
01

Case reconstruction

Extract dated facts, obligations, events and claims from case files, with every material fact tied back to its exact source.

02

Timeline + contradictions

Build a chronological matter timeline, identify corroborated events, and surface conflicting dates, statuses and accounts with severity.

03

Evidence gaps

Find documents, reports and other evidence referenced by the record but never actually supplied to the matter.

04

Case map

Bring facts, events, contradictions and evidence gaps together in one inspectable representation of the matter.

05

Deposition intelligence

Identify admissions, inconsistencies, hedging language and other material signals across transcripts and depositions.

02 / LEGAL INTELLIGENCE
06

Legal research

Answer legal questions against an indexed case-law corpus and rank relevant precedent against the issue being examined.

07

Citation verification

Check citations against the underlying case-law corpus and flag authorities where the citation, case or proposition does not match.

08

Formal legal tests

Evaluate each element of a defined legal test against the case facts separately, so the lawyer can inspect every step.

03 / CONTRACT INTELLIGENCE
09

Clause risk analysis

Extract clauses, classify their characteristics and score risk against the firm's configured rules and playbooks.

10

Redlines

Generate proposed replacement language from approved clause templates rather than inventing contractual language from scratch.

11

Portfolio intelligence

Roll contract risk, obligations, deadlines and renewal windows into a single portfolio view, including M&A diligence.

04 / STRATEGIC INTELLIGENCE
12

Outcome assessment

Estimate case outcomes from explicit weighted factors, expose the factor breakdown, and track calibration against real results.

13

Strategy builder

Structure the key issues, arguments and counterarguments around the matter's actual facts and legal framework.

05 / FIRM INTELLIGENCE
14

Institutional memory

Search the firm's memos, precedents and prior matters by question, with similar-case suggestions alongside the result.

15

Client intelligence

Roll multiple matters into a client-level view of risk, exposure and opportunities.

16

Practice operations

Track contractual deadlines and generate billing information from the matter activity already recorded by the system.

17

Firm controls

Apply firm-specific playbooks, severity overrides and a human-reviewed terminology glossary on top of the base intelligence engine.
